The current voyage

M.Tech thesis: Study of Coordination in an Infodemic Spread

In progress Shiv Nadar University, Chennai · 2025 – 2027

During an infodemic, misinformation travels through groups of users whose wording, timing and behaviour can move together. Measuring that coordination at scale is held back by the cost of hand-labelling user responses. The thesis proposes an automated, graph-assisted framework that labels how users respond to misinformation and then measures how strongly they act in a coordinated way.

Proposed architecture

  1. 1

    Data

    • Social-media misinformation and infodemic datasets
    • Cleaning and normalisation
    • A labelled stance seed set and a larger unlabelled pool
  2. 2

    Representation

    • Domain and topic identification on the unlabelled pool
    • Sentence-transformer embeddings for every post
  3. 3

    Graph-based labelling

    • Similarity and hashtag graph over posts
    • Label propagation from the seed set
    • Confidence and entropy filtering, then human verification
  4. 4

    Stance classification

    • Transformer stance classifier trained on the expanded set
    • Automatic annotation of the remaining data
  5. 5

    Coordination analysis

    • User and post coordination networks
    • Temporal and semantic coordination signals
    • A coordination score, compared across stances and events

Supervised by Dr. Sakthi Balan Muthiah. The work extends the research of Mr. Biswamitra Mitra on user-level coordinated spread and intervention of misinformation, and he also guides the current project. The architecture is a proposal and will change as experiments finish; code and results stay private until the work is complete.

Aug – Sep 2026 · SIH 2026, Team Jura Tempest

Hallucination-Resistant Multimodal Socratic Tutor

An offline tutor over NCERT textbooks (classes 1–12), stored as a chapter-to-chunk graph in Neo4j. Hybrid retrieval (BGE-M3 dense, full-text, graph and image search) is merged by weighted Reciprocal Rank Fusion and re-ranked with BGE-reranker-v2-m3. An evidence gate makes the tutor decline rather than invent an answer. Students ask by text or photo; the tutor replies with a hint and a guiding question, and SymPy checks maths answers.

Dec 2025

Human Protein Atlas Classification with Explainable AI

A 4-channel (RGBY) MobileNetV2 predicts which of 28 subcellular compartments a protein occupies, trained on 31,072 confocal microscopy images. Near-duplicate images are grouped before a group-aware split, per-class thresholds are tuned on validation, and Grad-CAM shows the evidence behind each prediction. Served by a FastAPI backend and a Next.js front end.

  • 0.571test macro F1
  • 0.949macro ROC-AUC
  • 4,636held-out images
  • PyTorch
  • MobileNetV2
  • Grad-CAM
  • FastAPI
  • Next.js

Paddy Yield Prediction

A regression pipeline on the UCI Paddy dataset (2,789 records) that audits leakage first: 451 duplicate rows and a post-harvest variable were removed, and features were cut from 69 to 44 with no loss in cross-validation.
